# Three Beds, Five Lives

An interactive Twine story exploring algorithmic ethics in medical triage.

## About
You are an ICU administrator during a pandemic. Configure an AI system to allocate three ICU beds among five critically ill patients. Your choices reveal how algorithms encode values and distribute harm.

## Play Online
[Play the story here](https://aria-x1.github.io/three-beds-five-lives/)

## Theme
Can algorithms fairly decide who receives life-saving medical care when not everyone can be saved?

## Course
COMP 380: Technology, Ethics, & Culture

---
Based on research examining COVID-19 triage protocols, algorithmic bias in healthcare, and principles of scarce resource allocation.
